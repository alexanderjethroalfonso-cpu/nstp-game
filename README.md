# TINDAHAN RUSH 🏪🍉

A loud, silly, offline tindahan game for one iPad in landscape. Kriste runs the sari-sari store and Helen runs the fruit stall. You play 5 days and try to end up with the biggest IPON.

## Play

Open `index.html`. Everything is in that one file: no internet, fonts, images or libraries needed.

**Put it on the iPad**

1. Host the folder on a website. GitHub Pages works (repo Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch, root folder). Free Pages needs a public repo.
2. On the iPad, open the link in Safari, then tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open the new home-screen icon **once while online**. The small `sw.js` saves a copy then, so the icon works in airplane mode afterward.

Opening `index.html` straight from the Files app only shows a preview, and the buttons may not work there. Use the home-screen way.

Turn the iPad sideways. Turn the silent switch off if you want sound. The 🔊 button mutes.

## How a day works (CHAOS EDITION ⚠️)

1. **Presyo:** pick MURA (lots of customers, small kita), SAKTO, or MAHAL (few customers, big kita).
2. **Rush (60s):** tap every item in each customer's bubble. Orders can have up to 3 items, and fast taps build a combo (SHEESH → GRABE → NAKAKALOKA).
   - Each item has limited stock (x6). When it hits **UBOS!**, tap it 5 times to restock.
   - From day 2 on, some customers pay with a big bill: pick the right **SUKLI** before the timer runs out. A wrong answer means you get scammed and lose that sale.
   - Fake **ads** pop up over the customers. Tap ✖ to close them. Tapping the ad itself costs ₱10.
   - 👸 **Ninang Galante** (the boss) shows up once a day with a 5-item order: double kita + ₱50.
   - Fill the 🔥 **HYPE** meter with fast sales, then tap it for 8 seconds of HYPER MODE (double kita).
   - Ignore Tita na Nagtatanong Lang 🧐: she never buys and costs you 3 seconds.
3. **Events:** day 2 gets one, days 3–5 get two each.
   - 🌧️ **ULAN** cuts customers in half.
   - 🚚 **Lipat Pwesto** costs 10 seconds.
   - ⚡ **BROWNOUT** turns everything dark except where you tap.
   - 🪰 **LANGAW** makes customers impatient until you swat the flies.
   - 🎉 **FIESTA** doubles the crowd, and ⭐ **Suki Day** means suki buy double.
4. **Hatiin:** drag your coins into the jars.
   - 📦 **PUHUNAN** buys tomorrow's stock. You need ₱100 for a full shelf.
   - ☂️ **EMERGENCY**: every ₱50 cancels one ULAN, Lipat Pwesto or BROWNOUT.
   - 🐷 **IPON** is your score.

There's a live announcer voice (the iPad's built-in voice, works offline), a fake livestream chat, and lots of noise. The 🔊 button mutes everything, voice included. There are no rapid full-screen flashes.

After day 5 you get a rank: Bagong Tindera → Sakto Lang Survivor → Suki Magnet → **IPON QUEEN**.

**2 PLAYERS:** Kriste plays all 5 days, passes the iPad to Helen, and the scores are shown side by side at the end. Both players get the same events on the same days.

High scores are saved on the device. If storage is blocked, the game still works but scores aren't kept.
